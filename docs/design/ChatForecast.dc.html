How this projection was worked out

Each month ahead is the average of the three months before it. January uses October, November and December (172, 168 and 180 units), which averages 173. February then uses November, December and the new January figure — and so on. Monthly demand is the total quantity ordered for this SKU that month.

Worth knowing: averaging smooths things out, so it will not follow a rising trend or a busy season. Demand here grew about 50% over the year but the projection stays flat. Read it as a safe floor rather than an exact figure. We chose it because you can check every number by hand.
