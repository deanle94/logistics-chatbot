Layout: where the chat lives Two of the three intelligence levels — diagnostic and predictive — live in the chat. That makes its placement a real decision, not a detail. Low-fi on purpose: pick the structure, not the pixels.
Option A — separate surfaces Built above

For Each surface gets the full width. The chat answer card can be 880px wide with a real chart in it — a forecast card needs that room. Matches the requirement’s own wording.
Against A reviewer who lands on the dashboard may never click through to the chat — and the chat is where the AI work is. One nav item is thin protection against that.
Option B — split canvas Sketch only
chart
chart
answer card
For The AI feature is unmissable — it is on screen from the first second. A manager can read a KPI and immediately interrogate it without losing the dashboard.
Against The chat column caps out near 500px. A forecast card carrying a chart, four values, a recommendation and a methodology note does not fit that width without becoming a scroll tunnel. It also breaks below ~1280px.
Recommendation — A, with the discoverability hole plugged Take Option A for the room, then fix its one weakness cheaply: put an always-visible ask bar at the top of the dashboard that submits straight into the chat. That buys Option B’s discoverability without paying its width cost. It is a few lines of UI, not a second layout.
